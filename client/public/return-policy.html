Return & Exchange Policy

At AVARONI (আভরণী), your utmost satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a transparent, easy 7-day return and exchange policy.

Home Return Policy

7-Day Window

Enjoy a full 7 calendar days from your parcel delivery date to inspect, try, and request a return or replacement.

Instant Exchange

Received the wrong size or color? Request an instant exchange for an alternate size or similar valued item.

Fast 5-7 Day Refunds

Once your returned item is verified, your refund is processed directly to your bKash, Nagad, or bank account.

General Return Guidelines

To ensure a seamless return or exchange process, please review the following criteria:

  • Item Condition: The item must be unworn, unwashed, undamaged, and in the exact condition you received it.
  • Original Packaging & Tags: All original brand tags, barcodes, garment polybags, and accessories must remain attached.
  • Proof of Purchase: Please provide your Order ID, invoice copy, or the contact phone number used during checkout.
  • Delivery Inspection: We strongly encourage inspecting parcel contents in front of the delivery agent when receiving cash on delivery.

Non-Returnable Items

Due to hygiene and quality standards, the following items are strictly non-returnable unless defective upon delivery:

  • Intimate wear, innerwear, and undergarments.
  • Custom-tailored, altered, or personalized clothing.
  • Clearance sale items marked as "Final Sale".

Refund & Processing Timeline

Once your returned parcel reaches our central fulfillment hub in Dhaka, our quality assurance team will inspect the item within 24–48 hours. Upon approval, your full product amount will be refunded within 5–7 business days via bKash, Nagad, or Bank Transfer.
