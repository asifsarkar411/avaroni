How do I place an order on AVARONI? Ordering
Placing an order is simple and takes less than 2 minutes:
  • Browse our categories (Women Dress, Ornament, Kids Zone) and click on any product you love.
  • Choose your desired size, color, or quantity and click "Add to Cart" or "Order Now".
  • Proceed to the cart/checkout page, enter your delivery address and contact number.
  • Select your preferred payment method (Cash on Delivery, bKash, Nagad, etc.) and click "Confirm Order".
What payment methods do you accept? Payment
We accept multiple secure payment options for your convenience:
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Pay in cash directly to the delivery rider when your package arrives.
  • bKash / Nagad / Rocket: Instant mobile wallet payment at checkout.
  • Credit / Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, and UnionPay.
How long does delivery take inside and outside Dhaka? Delivery
Our standard delivery timelines across Bangladesh are:
  • Inside Dhaka City: 24 to 48 hours (1 - 2 business days).
  • Dhaka Suburbs & Greater Dhaka: 2 to 3 business days.
  • Outside Dhaka (All 64 Districts): 3 to 5 business days via reputable courier partners (Steadfast, Pathao, RedX).
How can I track the live status of my parcel? Tracking
You can track your order in real-time by visiting our Track Order page. Simply input your Order ID (sent via SMS/Email) or your registered mobile number to see the step-by-step dispatch and courier status.
What is your Return and Exchange policy? Returns
We offer a 7-Day Hassle-Free Return & Exchange Policy:
  • If the item received is defective, incorrect in size/color, or damaged during transit, you can request an exchange or full refund within 7 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, with original tags and packaging intact.
  • You can submit a request directly via our Return Product Page or message us on WhatsApp.
Are the product photos 100% original and real? Quality
Yes, 100%! All our product photographs and videos are shot in-house with actual studio samples. What you see is exactly what you receive. Subtle color variations might rarely occur depending on your mobile or monitor screen settings.
How do I select the right size for dresses and kids wear? Sizing
Every product modal includes a detailed size guide (Bust, Length, Waist measurements in inches). If you are unsure between two sizes, feel free to chat with our stylists on WhatsApp at 01628628300 for personalized measurement guidance before ordering!
Can I cancel or modify my order after placing it? Ordering
Yes! As long as your order has not been handed over to the courier rider (status: Pending or Processing), you can modify your address, items, or cancel your order by calling our helpline 01628628300 or messaging on WhatsApp.
